## What this report is

FDA released roughly 18 GB of internal documents on its review of the Pfizer-BioNTech and Moderna COVID-19 vaccines under court order in *PHMPT v. FDA*. Many pages contain FOIA redactions — black-bar boxes labeled `(b)(N)` indicating the legal basis for withholding.

This report catalogs the **top 100 redactions** in the corpus that are most worth following up on — selected from a pool of **~64,374 non-routine markers** (after excluding Case Report Forms, which hold 99% of all markers, and `(b)(6)` personal-privacy redactions, which protect the names of individual trial participants and case writers).

Each entry below gives a senator's office or an attorney enough information to ask FDA, in writing, for the specific text behind that specific redaction — citing the Bates number, page, and surrounding context.
